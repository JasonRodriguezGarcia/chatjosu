## HelpChat Vision

### Principles

HelpChat helps a person to freely explain their situation and guide them towards one or more ONGs/associations/resources.

The user should not know internal tags or categories.

Desired feeling: speaking with a clear, respectful, and solution-oriented companion, not filling out a form.

Understand before recommending.

A conversation may contain several needs; do not design permanently around 1 message = 1 tag.

Conversation and verifiable data of ONGs must be separated.

Data of associations must be shown in structured components, not relying on Markdown generated by AI.

Voice and text will be equivalent inputs: voice → transcription → user review → submission as text.

Location is secondary context and modifiable.

Tone should be close, respectful, calm, and solution-oriented; do not pretend to be a person or emit diagnoses.

Mobile will be the primary reference; desktop can later use conversation + resources.

Privacy: in production, avoid storing literal text for analytics; prioritize province/location, category, date, provider/model, latency, results, and fallback.

### Out of Scope

Explicitly exclude:
- Files;
- Documents;
- Case tracking;
- Direct requests;
- Login/performance complex profiles;
- Calculation of aids or benefits;
- Administrative rules.

Mention only as future evolution:
- ONG → public resources → discovery of aids → pre-evaluation → deterministic calculations.
